The problem

Aquaculture operations still run on disconnected systems.

Environmental monitoring, mortality records, site maps, permits, inspections, inventory, and reporting are often scattered across spreadsheets, paper logs, PDFs, emails, and disconnected tools — making compliance, decision-making, and day-to-day operations harder than they should be.

Fragmented operations

Critical farm, site, and operational records are spread across multiple systems.

Manual reporting

Permits, movement records, inspections, and annual reports are often rebuilt by hand.

Limited visibility

Environmental monitoring, mortality events, inventory, and infrastructure data rarely exist in one place.

Administrative burden

Farm managers spend valuable time searching for information instead of managing production.

"The paperwork burden falls on the same person managing the water, the staff, and the harvest."
