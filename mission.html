Mission

Our Mission

VATE exists to simplify aquaculture operations through technology.

Aquaculture producers manage environmental conditions, infrastructure, inventory, compliance requirements, mortality events, and operational data across a growing number of disconnected systems.

We believe technology should make these operations easier to manage, not more complicated.

Our mission is to provide operational intelligence built specifically for aquaculture, helping producers improve visibility, reduce complexity, strengthen compliance, and make better decisions across their farms.

01

Atlantic Canada Aquaculture

Atlantic Canada's aquaculture industry is one of the most ecologically and economically significant in North America. From the tidal flats of Prince Edward Island to the cold inlets of Nova Scotia and New Brunswick, operators are producing high-quality shellfish and finfish under demanding environmental conditions — with little purpose-built technology to help them do it.

02

Operational Complexity

Aquaculture operations involve coordinated logistics across multiple lease sites, seasonal variability, equipment, personnel, and live animal management — all simultaneously. The information required to run these operations safely is scattered across paper logbooks, spreadsheets, email threads, and disconnected government portals. That complexity costs operators time, money, and clarity.

03

Compliance Burden

Operators navigate a dense web of federal and provincial obligations: DFO licences, CFIA movement permits, AAR reporting, biosecurity plans, water quality thresholds, and more. Keeping up with these requirements manually is error-prone and time-consuming. A missed deadline or an incomplete record can result in operational disruption and regulatory risk.

04

Environmental Monitoring

Water quality, temperature, dissolved oxygen, algae concentrations, and proximity data from CFIA monitoring stations all affect production decisions. Operators need this information contextualized against their specific sites — not buried in raw data feeds or government spreadsheets they have to interpret themselves.

05

Mortality and Production Challenges

Unexplained mortality events, growth underperformance, and stock condition anomalies are among the most costly challenges in aquaculture. Detecting these early requires connecting field observations, environmental conditions, and historical patterns — something that is nearly impossible without structured operational data.

06

Better Decisions Through Operational Intelligence

VATE was built to solve these problems. By connecting compliance, field operations, monitoring, mapping, and reporting into a single platform, we give operators the data layer they need to make faster, more confident decisions — and spend less time on administration. Our mission is to make Atlantic Canada aquaculture more resilient, more compliant, and more productive.
